# Result scope

The frozen policies are 25 selected P4-B policies (five original modes times
five seeds) plus five PD+Gravity extension policies.  The P4-B evidence records
15,000,000 training transitions across the full six-arm final corpus.  P5
strict nominal-only evidence is 9,750 episodes; the 1,950 PD+Gravity P5 episodes
are explicitly post-hoc exploratory and never included in confirmatory rankings.
